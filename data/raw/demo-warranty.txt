Demo store policy — Warranty support
This fictional retailer helps customers submit a warranty support request with proof of purchase, product serial number, and a description of the fault. Demo support responds within two business days. Manufacturer warranty coverage and eligibility are determined by the manufacturer; this policy does not promise free repairs, replacement, or a warranty duration. Accidental damage is not promised coverage by the demo store. This is a demo store policy, not Apple policy. We cannot check an individual device warranty or submit real service requests.
