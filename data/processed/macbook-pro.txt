Progress toward Apple 2030

Apple 2030 is our commitment to be carbon neutral for our entire carbon footprint. Our plan to decarbonize products is rigorous and focuses on transitioning to low-carbon electricity, designing with recycled and low-carbon materials, and prioritizing lower-carbon ways of shipping products, like ocean freight.
See Apple’s commitment
Materials
The 14‑inch MacBook Pro is made with 45% recycled content,12 including:
100% recycled aluminum in the enclosure
100% recycled cobalt and 95% recycled lithium in the battery
100% recycled copper in multiple printed circuit boards and a thermal module component
100% recycled gold plating and tin solder in all Apple-designed printed circuit boards
100% recycled rare earth elements in all magnets
80% or more recycled steel in the keyboard feature plate and speaker components
Energy
50% of manufacturing electricity for the 14-inch MacBook Pro is sourced from low-carbon electricity13
ENERGY STAR® certified14
Packaging
100% fiber-based packaging15
Waste
No established final assembly sites generate waste sent to landfill as part of Apple’s Zero Waste Program16
Smarter chemistry17
All of the materials used in Apple products, accessories, and packaging are covered by the requirements of our Regulated Substances Specification(PDF) opens in new tab, which was one of the first in industry to restrict the use of key substances of concern. See the latest Environmental Progress Report(PDF) opens in new tab to understand Apple’s recent efforts in phasing out these chemistries.

Progress toward Apple 2030
Apple 2030 is our commitment to be carbon neutral for our entire carbon footprint. Our plan to decarbonize products is rigorous and focuses on transitioning to low-carbon electricity, designing with recycled and low-carbon materials, and prioritizing lower-carbon ways of shipping products, like ocean freight.
See Apple’s commitment
Materials
The 16‑inch MacBook Pro is made with 45% recycled content,12 including:
100% recycled aluminum in the enclosure and thermal module stage
100% recycled cobalt and 95% recycled lithium in the battery
100% recycled copper in multiple printed circuit boards and thermal module components
100% recycled gold plating and tin solder in all Apple-designed printed circuit boards
100% recycled rare earth elements in all magnets
80% or more recycled steel in the keyboard feature plate and speaker components
Energy
50% of manufacturing electricity for the 16-inch MacBook Pro is sourced from low-carbon electricity13
